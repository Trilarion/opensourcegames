Dig through a pixel-art mine to uncover drawings, recordings and other work, then scroll back up the shaft to revisit earlier finds. The browser game uses a fictional greenhouse studio. In the local edition, recorded AI usage and project outputs determine how far the drill can go and what the player can uncover.
